## What is Ibra Studio?

Ibra Studio is a **website design offer** by Ibrahim. You show people ready-made **template designs** (gym, supermarket, clinic, restaurant, salon, real estate, shop, and more). Clients pick a style, then you:

1. **Customize a template** to their brand, or  
2. **Design something custom**, or  
3. **Build and publish a full site online**

Every demo template includes:
- English + Arabic  
- Sample photos (free stock, no watermark)  
- **Brand kit** (font names + color squares with hex codes)  
- Sample prices that switch **JOD ↔ USD**

### What's usually included
- Up to agreed number of pages (often 1-5 for starters)  
- Mobile-friendly layout  
- EN and/or AR content (client provides text or you place what they send)  
- Contact / WhatsApp buttons  
- 1-2 rounds of small revisions  

### What's usually NOT included (extra quote)
- Logo design from zero  
- Professional photography  
- Online payments / booking systems  
- Ongoing monthly maintenance  
- Complex apps or databases
